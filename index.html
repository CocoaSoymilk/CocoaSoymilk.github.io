Real2Sim2Real on a $100 Arm

SmolVLA on an SO-101 arm memorizes where the cube was in the demos. A MuJoCo twin calibrated from the robot's own logs, broad randomized data and DAgger raise sim generalization from 4% to 63%, and co-training starts to transfer to the real arm. An agentic baseline (Claude + calibrated twin + IK, verifying every step) places the cube from unseen positions. All of it runs on one 8 GB GPU.
